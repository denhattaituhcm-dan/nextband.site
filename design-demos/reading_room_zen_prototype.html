The Stanford Conversation & The 1952 Dale Carnegie Diploma

Trong thời đại thuật toán có thể soạn thảo văn bản trong tích tắc, Warren Buffett chỉ ra kỹ năng đòn bẩy vĩ đại nhất: Khả năng thấu cảm, diễn đạt rành mạch và gây ảnh hưởng bền vững giữa con người với con người.

Warren Buffett has spent decades dispensing simple advice that tends to age remarkably well.

In an era where artificial intelligence can draft emails, summarize corporate meetings, generate presentations in seconds, the ability to communicate as an authentic human has become a genuine competitive advantage.

Speaking to a Stanford graduate many years ago, Buffett offered this advice: “At your age, the best way you can improve yourself is to learn to communicate better. Your results in life will be magnified if you can communicate them better.”

The only diploma I hang in my office is the communications diploma I got from Dale Carnegie in 1952. That hundred-dollar course was the most valuable investment I ever made.
